# Phase 5.2: interpreting submitted ranged attacks

November 10, 2026 (America/Chicago).

## Finding

The saved dice verification was preserving the player's successes. The missing link was between that verified result and combat interpretation:

- `Attack: Longbow` is a roll label, not a canonical skill name. Its fallback retrieval query asked broadly about “ranged or melee attack” and optional effects; it did not require the weapon table, the success threshold and the damage rule together.
- The character's weapon projection exposed Gear Bonus but omitted the actual `dmg` field and Range. A caller-supplied `metadata.weaponDamage` existed, but was not sufficient authoritative evidence of character/official damage.
- Protected instructions correctly prohibited premature hits and automatic optional-success spending, but left positive attack interpretation to free-form narration. “Matched action is not proof of a hit” did not distinguish an already successful attack roll from a pending legal defense or armor roll. The zero-success safeguard alone did not fix that omission.

This explains the application-level gap reproduced by a deliberately unhelpful provider that leaves an arrow in flight. No original production model trace or live campaign database was attached, so the model's internal reasoning is not claimed as known.

## Resulting behavior

The existing Submit to GM path still verifies the saved roll ID, faces, mathematical success count, character, fingerprint and push consequences. It now attaches a read-only ranged-attack interpretation grounded in authorized rule passages and the recorded weapon.

A successful ordinary Marksmanship roll is acknowledged immediately when the evidence establishes the hit threshold. For a one-success Longbow attack with matching recorded/official Damage 1, the response states **Successful attack roll** and **Base weapon damage: 1**. It then identifies the specific remaining defense/armor fact, instead of suspending the attack's success as an arrow still flying.

Three successes establish two additional successes. Damage 3 is offered as the maximum ordinary damage option when both are chosen for damage; allocation stays null until the player explicitly chooses. One success has no optional additional success to allocate, so its base damage can proceed directly after any legal defense is addressed. Zero successes miss when the official threshold is evidenced; the prior safe zero-success response remains available if source evidence is missing.

The result separates:

1. Attack-roll success/miss.
2. Legal, already-declared defensive response and its actual result.
3. Confirmed hit, selected damage, protection roll and resulting damage.

Verified mechanical responses are generated by the application, so the narrator cannot ignore that success or invent a numeric enemy result in this path. They use the existing chat/request persistence and cost no paid AI call. Ordinary narration resumes through the existing GM pipeline. This is a read-only attack calculation, not an NPC turn executor or health tracker.

## Weapon and source evidence

The existing canonical character projection now includes saved weapon Damage and Range. The attack uses its server-captured weapon-row snapshot when bound to a weapon; legacy unbound results may use one unambiguous matching current row. It never creates a default weapon or silently chooses among duplicate rows. An invalid snapshot/label mismatch stays unresolved. The first saved submission snapshot pins the selected weapon for retries.

Official weapon rows, extra-success damage and the hit threshold reuse the established mechanical-fact extractor/validator. The ordinary threshold keeps both the general-success citation and the ranged skill-roll citation when that bridge is required. Recorded damage must agree with authorized official evidence or an applicable supported house override; missing/conflicting fields require review. Client `metadata.weaponDamage` never supplies the trusted damage input. A regression deliberately supplies 99 there while the actual row/table establish 1.

Retrieval preserves an explicit source constraint from a matched declaration. It performs the focused equipment/damage query, the bounded defense/protection query, and at most one targeted authorized PDF lookup when the actual armor procedure is missing. Selection is deduplicated, capped at eight passages and 5,000 estimated local evidence tokens. If the core threshold is unverified, it retains the original budgeted retrieval/fallback instead of expanding a paid-model prompt. An omitted relevant house override is flagged for review. Unsupported defense/protection overrides do not silently become the official rule.

Actual fifth-printing evidence exercised by the suite:

| --- | --- |
| Player's Handbook 46 | General skill-roll success threshold |
| Player's Handbook 102 | Marksmanship/Shoot, extra damage, Dodge/Parry, pre-declaration and weapon-talent exception |

No page offset or weapon statistic is hardcoded into the calculation. The actual Heavy Crossbow test verifies Damage 3, distinct from Longbow Damage 1. Synthetic crossbow fixtures deliberately use a different rating and are labeled accordingly.

## Defense and armor without autonomous NPC combat

The application does not invent an NPC action, shield, armor rating or dice result. Target facts not already supplied need manual adjudication in this phase. The accepted chat statements are deliberately explicit; arbitrary Journal prose and NPC dialogue are not mined for statistics.

Examples after a saved attack submission:

```text
No defense attempted. Target is unarmored.
```

```text
Target attempts to dodge. A defensive action is available.
Defense was declared before the attack roll.
Defense roll: one success.
```

```text
No defense is available. I spend all additional successes on damage.
Armor rating: 3. Armor roll: one success.
```

Use actual values/results; these are input-format examples, not supplied enemy statistics. “I spend only one extra success increasing damage.” and “I spend zero additional successes on damage.” select partial/zero allocation. Questions such as “Can I spend one extra success?” do not spend anything. Armor questions likewise do not establish an unarmored target.

Dodge/Parry must have an available action and have been declared before the attack roll. The source-backed ranged Parry rule includes a shield **or** a Parrying weapon with the relevant weapon talent rank required by the passage. An explicitly illegal response is not replaced with a free alternate defense. A legal defense waits for its actual reported roll; its successes cancel attack successes according to the retrieved rule, then remaining damage options are recalculated. A defense with zero successes does not automatically work.

No available/attempted legal defense allows the attack to proceed to armor. Unknown armor remains unknown. Armor Rating is not subtracted as flat protection: the actual reported protection successes reduce damage. Explicitly reported cover requires its own source-backed applicability and is processed before armor; if cover absorbs everything, an unnecessary armor roll is not requested. Neither armor/cover degradation nor injuries are applied automatically.

These target inputs/results remain labeled **player-reported manual adjudication**, not server-generated NPC rolls or automatically approved canon. Follow-ups reverify the original saved attack, reuse existing AI request snapshots, and retain the original success count. A follow-up with an ambiguous roll selection asks which saved roll; its ID can be included from Recent Dice Rolls. No second attack roll is created or requested.

Later GM context carries the latest compact adjudicated result instead of replaying the initial “defense unknown” state. It omits source passage bodies from recent-roll summaries. Existing owner checks apply before reading private weapon/roll context, and public saved-context previews redact the new interpretation alongside actor/preparation/roll data.

Manual facts use explicit supported statements, not a general natural-language NPC rules engine. NPC identities, action budgets, talents and target rolls are not independently verified against a new NPC database. Unrelated/ambiguous prose still uses the existing GM guidance and may need clarification. Unmodified ordinary ranged attacks are the implemented calculation scope; special attacks, arbitrary talents, conditional house effects and full tactical legality require further adjudication. Unrecorded situational cover/modifiers are not silently certified absent; the ordinary result is conditional on the supplied facts.

The application calculates and reports consequences; it does not apply damage to character sheets, choose NPC tactics, generate enemy dice, consume supplies, advance time, change conditions or commit campaign canon. Existing push consequences are not repeated. No migration, general authentication bypass, external dependency, autonomous NPC combat, journey automation or image integration was added. Both licensed PDFs, extracts, private databases and user images remain outside Git.
